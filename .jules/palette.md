## 2025-02-28 - Focus and Labels for Interactive Editor Controls
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons used for editor controls (like "Minimize/Maximize" and "Reset changes") frequently lack clear labeling and explicit keyboard focus states, making them difficult to use for keyboard navigators and screen reader users. Simply adding standard generic attributes may result in poor context.
**Action:** When creating or modifying complex interactive components with icon-only controls, always provide explicit `aria-label` attributes and enforce keyboard focus visibility with classes like `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-maya-500 focus:outline-none`. For textareas acting as custom editors, apply an `aria-label` that includes the dynamic title of the section (e.g., `Edit ${title} script`) for better screen reader context.
